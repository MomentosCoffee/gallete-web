The 2-minute ritual

How to make your matcha latte like a pro

1

Sift 5 g of matcha into a bowl.

2

Add 15 mL room-temperature water and whisk into a smooth paste.

3

Add 40 mL water (below 80°C / 176°F) and whisk in an “M” or “W” motion until foamy.

4

Fill a glass with 150 mL of your favorite milk, ice and sweetener (optional).

5

Pour the matcha over the milk, stir, and enjoy.

Gali's tipAlways sift before whisking, and never use boiling water, that's the secret to a smooth, foamy matcha.
